Frequently asked questions

How much does training cost?

The cost depends on the branch and the chosen plan. Standard plans start at 29 500 ₸/month for 2 lessons a week of 60 minutes. Prices are slightly higher at the Gagarin Park and Debut branches. See the details in the “Prices and memberships” section.

Which branches have Kazakh-speaking coaches?

Kazakh-speaking coaches work at the Debut, Zhandosov and Almaty Arena branches.

Do you offer private lessons?

Private lessons are only given as a trial lesson or when preparing for major championships (10 000 ₸/hour). For beginners and continuing students we recommend the group format — it is more effective for development.

What is the class schedule?

Classes are held twice a week for 60 minutes (90 or 120 minutes for ranked players). Groups run Mon/Wed and Tue/Thu: 9:00–18:00, Sat/Sun: 9:00–13:00. Friday is a day off.

Can we attend once a week?

No, this format isn’t offered. Once-a-week lessons don’t give the necessary results and practice. Progress requires at least 2 lessons a week.

How do tournaments work and when can my child take part?

Tournaments are held every weekend at 5 branches. A child can take part after completing the first stage of training and passing an exam with the coach. Up to 4 free tournaments a month are included in the membership.

Can I buy a partial membership?

No, the membership is purchased in full. Attendance is only possible according to the set schedule.

Do you teach adults?

Yes, adult classes are held at the Debut branch (71 Kabanbay Batyr St.) from 19:00 to 21:00. One lesson costs 5 000 ₸. The trial lesson is free.

Do you accept “Damu Bala”?

No, we don’t have that program. We work with our own Chess Empire championship method.

Is there free training?

Only the trial lesson (30 minutes) is free. Training is paid — from 29 500 ₸/month. Discounts are available for large families and children with disabilities.

Do you create the personal account, or do we need to register ourselves?

The personal account is created after the first lesson together with the coach. We’ll help you register and explain how to use it.
